<div class="lesson-no">Section 12c · The offer formula</div>

# Skill → Problem → Outcome → Offer → Proof

<div class="qa">
<div class="qa-item">
<div class="qa-q">"I know n8n and ChatGPT."</div>
<div class="qa-line"><span class="qa-tag weak">Weak</span><span class="qa-why">A skill list. Says nothing about the outcome a client gets.</span></div>
</div>
<div class="qa-item">
<div class="qa-q">"I build an AI lead-follow-up system that responds to new leads within minutes and automatically routes qualified leads to your sales team."</div>
<div class="qa-line"><span class="qa-tag power">Strong</span><span class="qa-why">A named outcome, for a specific problem, that a client can picture immediately.</span></div>
</div>
</div>

Note: Read both lines aloud and let the contrast land — this single reframe is often the difference between a stalled freelancer and a booked one.

<div class="lesson-no">Section 12d · First client</div>

# Getting Your First Client

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">1</div><div class="step-t">Pick one niche</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">2</div><div class="step-t">Pick one painful problem</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">3</div><div class="step-t">Build one demo</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">4</div><div class="step-t">Package it as a simple offer</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">5</div><div class="step-t">Contact potential clients</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">6</div><div class="step-t">Show the demo instead of only talking about AI</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">7</div><div class="step-t">Start with a small implementation</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-n">8</div><div class="step-t">Turn the result into a case study</div></div>
</div>

Note: Step 6 is the one worth emphasizing hardest — a 60-second working demo beats a 10-minute AI pitch every time.

<div class="lesson-no">Section 12e · Final challenge</div>

# Pick One Problem You Can Solve This Week

<p class="rule"><b>Final message</b> You don't need to master every AI tool. You need to understand the fundamentals, learn how to communicate with AI, choose the right tools, build useful systems, and connect those systems to real problems.</p>

Note: Ask the room to actually name their one problem out loud or in chat — this is the commitment moment before the close, don't skip it.
